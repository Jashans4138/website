Our Story

Born from a shared passion for fashion and technology, OUTFIX began as a spark of inspiration in a classroom and blossomed into a vibrant digital boutique.

Jashan and Avneet transformed their academic vision into a cutting-edge platform where style meets innovation, offering:

  • 50+ handpicked styles to express your unique fashion sense
  • Smart search that understands your style preferences
  • Personalized accounts to curate your fashion journey
  • Seamless checkout for effortless shopping
